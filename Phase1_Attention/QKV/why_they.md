# diff b/w key and value
Real-Life Analogy (This one usually makes it click)

Imagine you're building a company.

Each employee has a full profile.

Sudharshan

↓

Name

Skills

Experience

Projects

Education

Certificates

# Now suppose HR asks:

# "Who knows PyTorch?"  Q

Do they compare your entire profile?

No.

They extract only:

# Skills  K

 That's the Key.

Now suppose you ask:

# "Who here can help me build an LLM?"  Q

Your question becomes a Query.

If HR finds a match...

# Do they send only the word "PyTorch"?

No.

They send your entire expertise.

That's the Value.

So we have:

#  Full Profile     V

       ↓

# Query  → How I search

# Key    → How others find me

# Value  → What I contribute
# 🤯 THIS is why V is different from K

Most beginners think:

"Why not use K as the output?"

Imagine HR says:

"We found Sudharshan."

Should they return:

Skills: PyTorch

or

Everything Sudharshan knows

Obviously the second.

That's why:

Key

↓

Used only for matching

Value

↓

Used for information transfer
